Privacy policy

KG rclone is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to other people and collects no data from them.

It can read and change the files in that account's Google Drive. Access tokens are kept on the owner's own computers.

Files and file details are used only for the owner's own work. The owner's tools may send file names and folder names to an AI model provider the owner has chosen, to help search those files. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google account's third-party connections page.
